How to study

Use active recall instead of just rereading.

01 · Observe

Study silhouette, head shape, ears, coat, proportions, gait clues, and breed purpose before reading the name.

02 · Retrieve

Identify breeds from visual clues, then explain your reasoning. Retrieval strengthens memory more than recognition alone.

03 · Connect

Link appearance and breed history to temperament, exercise needs, common disease risks, and veterinary considerations.

Veterinary learning checklist

Use breed predispositions as study associations, not diagnostic shortcuts.
Clinical decisions require the individual patient's history, examination, diagnostic testing, and professional veterinary judgment.
  • Connect breed → likely phenotype → possible disease predisposition.
  • Ask what a breed's original function might imply for exercise, behavior, handling, and enrichment.
  • Look for coat, ear, skin, dental, orthopedic, and cardiac considerations where relevant.
  • Distinguish inherited predisposition from conditions caused by environment, management, age, or unrelated disease.
